## Selected work

### [`Network Traffic Attack Classification`](https://github.com/ichrakhamdi/DeepLearningProject) · Spotlight

An end-to-end deep learning pipeline that detects and classifies **cyberattacks in network traffic**. It compares four architectures on the same data: **LSTM**, **Transformer**, a hybrid **CNN-Transformer** and **FT-Transformer**. Data preparation, configurable experiment runners, evaluation and artifact logging are all part of the pipeline.

`Python` · `TensorFlow` · `LSTM` · `Transformers` · `Intrusion detection` · `Imbalanced learning` · `scikit-learn`

[![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-Repository-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/ichrakhamdi/DeepLearningProject)

### [`Mammogram Segmentation`](https://github.com/ichrakhamdi/mammogram_segmentation) · Medical imaging

A comparative study of breast-cancer segmentation methods: a **U-Net CNN** against two classical approaches, **Fuzzy C-Means** and **Expectation-Maximization**.

`Python` · `U-Net` · `Computer Vision` · `Fuzzy C-Means` · `EM`

[![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-Repository-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/ichrakhamdi/mammogram_segmentation)

### [`Distributed Flow-Shop Scheduling with Workers`](https://github.com/ichrakhamdi/Distributed-Flow-shop-Scheduling-Problem-with-worker-Project) · Optimization

A heuristic for the **Distributed Permutation Flow-Shop Scheduling Problem with worker flexibility (DPFSPw)**. Jobs are spread across several factories, each needing both a machine and an available worker, and the goal is to minimize makespan. Based on research presented at ICCCI 2022.

`Python` · `Heuristics` · `Scheduling` · `Operations Research`

[![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-Repository-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/ichrakhamdi/Distributed-Flow-shop-Scheduling-Problem-with-worker-Project)
